## [1.0.0] - 2026-05-25

### Added
- Gradio-based web interface for Whisper transcription
- Microphone recording support
- Single and batch file upload (WAV, MP3, etc.)
- Model selection (tiny, base, small, medium, large, turbo)
- Language selection with auto-detect option
- Supported languages: English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Chinese, Japanese
- Export transcriptions as TXT (single file) or CSV (batch)
- Progress tracking during transcription
- Dark theme with custom styling
- 100% local processing — no internet required after setup
